Part 2

A launch plan outline

New capability

Trusted Voice Profiles

Allow NHS trusts to use a familiar, approved voice or persona for Agentic Voice, making patient conversations feel more familiar and human.

Patients are clearly informed they are speaking with an automated assistant.

Objective

Launch the capability with one NHS lighthouse partner, demonstrate its value in a real workflow, and create qualified demand from existing NHS customers.

Audience

Primary: NHS COOs & operational leaders

Key stakeholders

Clinical / Medical Clinical Safety CIO / IG Finance Innovation
Phasing
Phase 01 · Validate

Make it work before we make noise.

  • Align Product, Engineering and Marketing on the capability
  • Test voice, workflow and integration
  • Develop initial messaging and demo
  • Internal review and refine
Phase 02 · Prove

Take it to an existing NHS partner.

  • Identify one suitable NHS trust
  • Test the capability within a real workflow
  • Capture feedback and patient/team response
  • With permission, record a real conversation; otherwise use a realistic demo
  • Build a short launch story around the use case and outcome
